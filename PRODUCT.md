# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are one or two parents running a small Tupperware-style home business. They are comfortable with basic phone, iPad, laptop, and internet use but are not technical. A technical family member can perform one-time setup.

## Product Purpose

Home Inventory records sales and keeps stock accurate for roughly 1,000 products. Success means the parents can complete routine sales, deliveries, counts, and corrections without instruction or dependence on one device.

## Positioning

A deliberately small, hosted inventory tool that uses familiar home-business language and workflows instead of retail, warehouse, or accounting terminology.

## Operating Context

Users work across phones, iPads, and laptops, sometimes concurrently. Routine actions are Sell, Stock arrived, Count stock, Fix a mistake, product maintenance, sale cancellation, and data export. Currency is INR and UPI is the default payment method.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One shared password for one or two users; no registration, roles, or user administration.
- Near-real-time synchronization within roughly 30 seconds is sufficient.
- Hosted at no monetary cost within current free-tier limits.
- Approximately 1,000 products; product search must remain fast and comprehensible.
- Stock and money changes preserve history and are transactionally safe.
- Phase 0 currently uses sample, in-memory data and is not production-ready.

## Brand Commitments

The interface may retain a subtle connection to an Indian home business, but the incumbent paper-ledger appearance is not binding. The approved direction is clean, light, restrained, spacious, and familiar: blue is the only strong accent, icons are simple line drawings, and decorative imagery, logos, and loud multicolour surfaces are avoided. The product voice must remain plain, respectful, and nontechnical.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a functioning Phase 0 prototype, representative seed products, documented workflows, and unit tests. It has no final logo, product photography, customer evidence, or approved production catalogue.

## Product Principles

- Make the next routine action unmistakable.
- Prefer familiar language over inventory jargon.
- Prevent mistakes and make recovery obvious.
- Adapt intentionally to each device size.
- Add features only after observed user need.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Design for older, nontechnical users: minimum 16px body text, at least 44px touch targets, visible focus, keyboard support, no hover-only behavior, and text labels for primary actions.
